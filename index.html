Demo script (about 10 minutes)

  1. Press Reset demo data below. Open the Data Flow Monitor as presenter on one side of the screen.
  2. Plant Time System (timeoffice): send the daily file. An absence alert appears for Kiran Salunkhe.
  3. Register Upload Portal (coordinator): upload September; one unknown ticket is rejected.
  4. TeamLease Partner Portal (teamlease): import two joiners into EC.
  5. Line Incident Reporting (supervisor): report mobile use for Akash Pawar.
  6. Line Manager Portal (anaik): rate Ganesh Wagh and Akash Pawar in Team Rater or the full form, then sign and send.
  7. Scoring Agent Console (hrisadmin): run the agent step by step.
  8. HoD Portal (piyer, dmehta): sign forms; decide Vishal Gaikwad’s Month 12 conversion.
  9. Plant HR Portal (nsharma): confirm the final decision; the EC job change shows in the log.
